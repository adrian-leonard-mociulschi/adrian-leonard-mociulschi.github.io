Virtual Box

Welcome to the a digital space dedicated to the international writings of Adrian Leonard Mociulschi, exploring the intersections of culture, technology, society, and strategic change. This page brings together a selection of articles published in international media, with a particular focus on Nine O’Clock, where he is a regular contributor. Covering themes such as Artificial Intelligence, technoculture, finance, geopolitics, education, and scientific research, these texts examine the profound transformations of the contemporary world through a perspective grounded in meaning, responsibility, and long-term human development.

🔗 Beyond Nostalgia: Why Artemis II Marks a Different Kind of Return

Nine O'Clock • Issued on July 21, 2026

An essay exploring the significance of NASA's Artemis II mission as humanity's return to deep space. Moving beyond Apollo-era nostalgia, it examines lunar exploration, space infrastructure, and the long-term vision of establishing a sustainable human presence beyond Earth.

🔗 Between Discipline and Calling: A Conversation with TAROM Boeing 737 First Officer George Ancuța

Nine O'Clock • Issued on July 19, 2026

Published on the occasion of Romanian Aviation Day, this interview with TAROM Boeing 737 First Officer George Ancuța explores the realities of commercial aviation beyond the cockpit door. From pre-flight preparation and flight safety to the beauty of sunsets at cruising altitude and the responsibility of carrying hundreds of passengers, the conversation offers a human perspective on discipline, professionalism, and the enduring calling of flight.

🔗 Romania, OECD, and Financial Education

Nine O'Clock • Issued on May 25, 2026

In this interview, Prof. Dr. Marian Siminică, Executive Director of ISF, discusses Romania’s OECD accession process, the role of financial education in economic development, the impact of technology on financial literacy, and the skills needed to build resilience in an increasingly complex and digitalized economy. The conversation explores how education, responsibility, and long-term planning can help prepare both young people and the wider public for the financial challenges of the future.

🔗 From Air Defense to Space Security: Romania at the Threshold of a New Strategic Domain

Nine O'Clock • Issued on May 22, 2026

An exclusive Nine O’Clock interview with General (Ret.) Ștefan Dănilă exploring the transition from air defense to space security, the strategic implications of Artemis II, the future of space governance, and Romania’s role in the emerging space domain. The discussion examines human performance under extreme acceleration, the evolution of aerospace operations, and the importance of technological integration within European and allied space frameworks.

🔗 At That Hour, the Mountain No Longer Belongs to Maps

Nine O'Clock • Issued on May 04, 2026

A literary and strategic portrait of Brigadier General Cristian Teglaș, tracing the journey from the mountain front line to the halls of the U.S. Army War College. Blending military leadership, strategic thought, NATO interoperability, and the ethics of command, the text explores the quiet discipline through which responsibility becomes action and authority emerges from experience rather than display.

🔗 The Tradition of Mărțișor and the Quiet Brilliance of Liana Alexandra

Nine O'Clock • Issued on February 28, 2026

A reflection on the Romanian tradition of Mărțișor and the enduring legacy of composer Liana Alexandra. Through the symbolism of spring renewal, cultural memory, and artistic excellence, this essay explores how quiet dedication, creativity, and musical craftsmanship continue to shape the cultural life of Romania and beyond.
